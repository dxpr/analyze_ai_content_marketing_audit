Find Out Why Some Content Converts and Some Doesn't

You published 200 blog posts. Some drive signups, most don't, and nobody knows why. This module scores every piece of content against seven proven marketing factors - usability, actionability, business value, and more - so you stop guessing and start optimizing.

You need AI Content Marketing Audit if

  • Your content performs inconsistently and you can't pinpoint what separates winners from duds
  • You have a large content library that's never been systematically evaluated
  • Your marketing team makes content decisions based on gut feeling rather than measurable criteria
  • You want to benchmark new content against proven marketing principles before publishing

What You Get

  • Seven marketing factors scored per page

    Each content entity is evaluated on Usability, Knowledge Level, Actionability, Accuracy, Business Value, Messaging, and Brand Voice Fit - with scores from -1.0 (poor) to +1.0 (excellent).

  • Quantitative and qualitative analysis

    Numeric scoring for measurable factors plus categorical classification for subjective ones - giving you both the numbers and the narrative.

  • Fully customizable factors

    Add your own marketing factors, remove ones that don't apply, reorder by priority, and define custom options for qualitative dimensions.

  • Batch analysis for content audits

    Score your entire content library to identify low-performing content that needs rewriting, high performers worth promoting, and patterns across content types.

  • AI Coding Assistant Integration

    Marketing audit analysis is available to AI coding assistants through the Analyze module's built-in Agent Skills file. Run drush analyze:setup-ai to enable, then ask naturally:

    • "Audit content marketing quality on all articles"
    • "Check the usability score for landing pages"
    • "Run a marketing audit on all published content"

    Compatible with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and other tools supporting the standard.

Getting Started

  1. Set up an AI provider at /admin/config/ai/providers
  2. Review and customize audit factors at /admin/config/analyze/content-marketing-audit
  3. Enable the analyzer per content type at /admin/config/content/analyze-settings
  4. Open any content entity's Analyze tab to see the scores
