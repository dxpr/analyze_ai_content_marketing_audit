Professional Content Marketing Analysis Powered by AI

Transform your content strategy with comprehensive AI-powered marketing analysis based on proven content strategy principles, evaluating five essential factors that drive engagement and business results.

You need Content Marketing Audit if

  • You want data-driven insights into your content's marketing effectiveness
  • Your content performs inconsistently and you need to identify why
  • You need systematic evaluation of large content volumes
  • You want to benchmark content against proven marketing principles

Comprehensive Marketing Analysis

  • Dual Analysis Approach

    Multi-factor marketing analysis with both quantitative scoring (-1.0 to 1.0) and qualitative categorical classification, with customizable factor weighting and ordering.

  • Five Core Marketing Factors

    Pre-configured analysis dimensions based on content strategy best practices:

    • Usability - Content ease of understanding and action
    • Knowledge Level - Expertise and authority demonstration
    • Actionability - Clear next steps and actionable insights
    • Accuracy - Factual correctness and currency
    • Funnel Stage - Marketing funnel targeting (Awareness/Consideration/Decision/Retention)
  • Flexible Factor Management

    Intuitive admin interface for adding, editing, and deleting custom marketing factors to match your organization's needs.

  • Enterprise Batch Processing

    Efficiently analyze large volumes of existing content with intelligent batch processing capabilities and visual reporting.

Getting Started

  1. Configure AI provider settings at /admin/config/ai/providers
  2. Manage audit factors at /admin/config/analyze/content-marketing-audit
  3. Enable/disable the analyzer per content type at /admin/config/content/analyze-settings
  4. Configure permissions at /admin/people/permissions#module-analyze_ai_content_marketing_audit
  5. Access content marketing audit through the Analyze tab on content
  6. Run batch analysis at /admin/config/analyze/content-marketing-audit/batch
